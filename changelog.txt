Version: 0.7.0
Date: 06. 10. 2026
  Features:
    - /forge-export writes a "bonuses" section into environment.json: the force-wide sums the
      in-game bonus screen shows (inserter and bulk inserter capacity, belt stack size, mining and
      lab productivity, robot speed, storage and battery, and the rest) by their Lua API names.
    - Per-recipe productivity from research, and per-category ammo damage, gun speed and turret
      attack modifiers, where non-zero.
  Info:
    - Bonuses are read from the force rather than summed from technologies, because not all of
      them come from research: the bulk inserter technology itself raises bulk inserter capacity.
    - A bonus the running game version does not have is left out instead of failing the export.
    - Added this changelog.
